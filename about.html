About Us

Sputnik Cosmotics is a student-led astronomical club dedicated to exploring the vast cosmos, encouraging curiosity about the universe, and building a community of stargazers, learners, and dreamers. We conduct events, publish cosmic articles, and maintain tools for space enthusiasts of all levels.
